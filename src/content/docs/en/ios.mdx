The Scribe for Documents iOS app enables seamless access to document remediation directly from your iPhone or iPad.
[Download Scribe for Documents on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/scribe-for-documents/id6791628388)

## Adding Documents

Tap "Add a Document" to take a photo of a document with your camera, choose a file from your device, or enter a web address. Supported file types include PDF, Word, PowerPoint, and common image formats. You can also share files directly to Scribe from other apps like Files, Mail, or Safari using the share sheet.

## Taking Photos of Pages

Scribe allows you to take a photo of a page using the camera on your device. Once you activate "Take a Photo," the camera will automatically begin looking for a page. Hold your device over the center of the document, as close as possible, then slowly lift it until it is roughly 6 inches away from the page. Scribe will tell you whether the page is in view, too far away, or partly cut off.

Automatic capture is enabled by default. This means that when Scribe recognizes that the page is in view, VoiceOver will tell you to hold still, and Scribe will then take the picture automatically.

You can also capture a page manually in several ways:

1. Press either volume button. This lets you take the photo without moving the device.
1. Activate the "Take Photo" button.
1. When VoiceOver is on, perform a two-finger double-tap anywhere on the screen.

Once the photo is taken, Scribe shows the page and the text it recognized. If you are satisfied with the recognition and only intend to capture a single page, activate "Finish Scanning". If you want to rescan the page, activate "Try This Page Again".

If you have more pages to scan, activate "Add Another Page" or perform the VoiceOver two-finger double-tap gesture. Scan as many pages as you need, then activate "Finish Scanning".

To check your pages, delete one taken by mistake, or change their order, activate "Pages". Each page is listed with the first line of its text. Pages with no text are marked, since they are usually photos taken by accident, so you can easily delete or retake them.

## Live Scan

Live Scan allows you to quickly scan and read text using nothing but your device’s camera. This is very useful for quickly sorting through mail, flipping through pages, and the like. Simply aim your camera at a page, box, envelope, etc., and Scribe will automatically read any text it recognizes. The text will appear on screen as it is read aloud. Use the “Repeat” button to repeat the last recognized text, and the “Copy” button to copy it to the clipboard.

To pause recognition, use the “Pause Scanning” button or VoiceOver’s two-finger double-tap gesture. While paused, the screen lists everything read since you opened Live Scan, newest first. This can come in handy if something went by too quickly. This screen features a search field for finding specific information, such as a phone number. You can copy a selected item’s text with VoiceOver’s Actions rotor, or by touching and holding it. Activating the “Resume Scanning” button or performing a two-finger double-tap will resume scanning. A subsequent pause will list any new items along with the earlier ones. The scan history is cleared when you completely close Live Scan.

Live Scan does not require any page credits, as it uses on-device recognition exclusively.

## Conversion Settings

Scribe converts documents to accessible formats including HTML, DAISY, EPUB, MP3 audio, and Braille. You can initiate a conversion from the "Document Options" menu while inside a document. After selecting a format, you can customize the document's language, image descriptions, math notation support, and text-to-speech settings. Once you've adjusted the settings to your liking, activate the "Convert" button.

To set your preferred defaults for all new documents, go to the "Account" tab, then select "Default Conversion Settings".

## Reading and Downloading

Tap any converted document to open it in the built-in accessible reader. From there, you can use the "Document Options" menu to download other available formats such as DAISY, EPUB, MP3, or Braille.
